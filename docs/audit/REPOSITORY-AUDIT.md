## Product and implementation

Ternuxx uses Native Termux as its host and control plane, with optional Debian PRoot workloads. The shared typed management service fronts CLI, TUI, authenticated loopback Web, and GTK clients; individual client coverage and remaining acceptance are listed in the [capability matrix](../architecture/CAPABILITY-MATRIX.md). P11 Debian restoration is closed. The latest recorded live GUI/guest process observation is dated 2026-10-24 13:53 UTC; it reported Native display `:1`, no standalone Debian GUI on `:0` or nested `:2`, and an installed Debian Trixie guest. This report does not claim that snapshot as a fresh check. Current state and process-ownership limits are in [CURRENT_STATE](../../CURRENT_STATE.md) and the [live reconciliation record](../evidence/2026-10-24-live-state-reconciliation.md).

CPU inference is bounded and workload-verified for the pinned local model. Vulkan inference has passed bounded Adreno 825 offload runs using an experimental Mesa/Freedreno ICD. Experimental HTP inference has passed bounded SM8735/HTP0 runs with full delegation and no silent CPU fallback; tested short prompts were slower than CPU. These results do not establish sustained thermal/memory behavior, clean-device installation, or general device support. The [feature matrix](../testing/FEATURE-MATRIX.yaml) and [P12 evidence](../evidence/2026-10-24-native-acceleration-live-check.md) hold the per-route claims.

Fresh guarded one-case checks now exist for CPU, Vulkan, and HTP; they remain short workload evidence, not repeated qualification. Details and limits are in [CURRENT_STATE](../../CURRENT_STATE.md) and the [single-case evidence](../evidence/2026-10-24-p12-single-case-backend-check.md).
