**Reviewed:** 2026-10-30

Distribution status changed on 2026-10-26: Ternuxx has a public experimental Mesa pre-release, [native-mesa-adreno825-2026.10.27](https://github.com/soobujmiah/ternuxx/releases/tag/native-mesa-adreno825-2026.10.27), with runtime and source assets. It is not a stable or production-qualified release, and its publication does not resolve the open component-license/provenance review. The reproducible HTP candidate remains a 90-day internal Actions artifact; the CPU/Vulkan `llama-cpp` package remains separately installed by Termux. The pinned model remains private device-local data and is not included in the Mesa release. These external terms are separate from any license for this repository.
