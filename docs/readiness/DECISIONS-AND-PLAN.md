## Cost and uncertainty

- **Current audit cost:** no purchases or paid services. Existing Git/GitHub/Actions usage observed; no charge estimate made.
- **Static hosting:** Cloudflare Pages current Free plan is a possible $0 static host within published limits. Domain, accountant/lawyer, payment provider and any paid hosting have no verified price in this audit.
- **Human time:** product qualification, legal chain-of-title review and website content are likely the dominant initial costs; no person-hour estimate is validated.
- **Unknowns:** device fleet compatibility, customer demand, effective support rate, number of hours per support case, service classification, payment availability, domain clearance, trademark conflicts, international liability, tax/VAT, license history legal effect, and release signing workflow.
