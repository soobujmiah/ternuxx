The pinned Qwen3 0.6B route and Termux llama.cpp runtime are available; Vulkan0 enumerates Adreno 825 and the experimental b11849 HTP0 route targets SM8735. A later guarded qualification completed three repeated eight-token Vulkan calls with 29/29 offload, minimum sampled `MemAvailable` about 4.41 GiB, and no leftover inference process; this is not sustained qualification. See the [HTP repeat evidence](docs/evidence/2026-10-24-p12-guarded-htp-repeat-check.md).
